# 4.3 View, approve, and take over on the phone

## Current status

- **Not implemented:** No mobile PWA, phone pairing, push, mobile approval, or remote takeover.
- **Current entry:** Only the same-machine web control surface. This step is a prerequisite for accepting Phase 4 as a whole.

## Goal

Let the user view, approve, and take over from a phone, while ensuring a lost phone can be revoked immediately and long-term primary authority is not stored on it.

## How to do it

1. Provide a phone web entry for today's tasks, items that need the user, and artifacts.
2. The approval page shows the real target, content, impact, and whether it can be undone; key actions require a second confirmation.
3. Support pause, takeover, and stop from the phone; takeover stops automatic operation immediately.
4. Pair with a short-lived code; the phone does not store long-term primary authority, and push content does not include sensitive text.
5. A lost phone can be revoked immediately; after revocation it cannot approve, take over, or view tasks.
6. When offline, offer read-only access and a clear state, then sync after reconnecting.
