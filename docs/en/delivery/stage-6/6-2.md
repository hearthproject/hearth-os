# 6.2 Revoke devices and migrate safely

## Goal

Let a lost, replaced, or migrated device be revoked and transferred safely; after revocation, the old device cannot approve, take over, or view data.

## How to do it

1. Provide an immediate revoke entry; after revocation, all sessions and approval authority become invalid at once.
2. After revocation, verify the old device cannot receive tasks, view artifacts, or perform actions.
3. Use a safe migration flow when replacing a device, then verify tasks, artifacts, memory, and authority afterwards.
4. Route tasks by device capability: desktop actions go to a device with desktop ability; the phone only views, approves, and takes over.
5. Record the revocation and migration so the user can review them.
6. Run a lost-device drill from revocation to confirming the old device is disabled.
