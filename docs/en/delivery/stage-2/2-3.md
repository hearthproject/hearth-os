# 2.3 Draft, approve, and write externally

## Current status

- **Implemented:** Drafts can be edited, saved, and discarded; submission creates a one-time, short-lived approval; approval writes to the local outbox through the effect ledger, or submits a real-send approval through a configured SMTP account and stores a receipt; rejection or expiry updates the draft state.
- **Still open:** Without SMTP, writes go only to the local `.hearth/outbox`; real-account behavior, bounce and spam semantics, and attachment selection are not accepted or implemented; calendar and other external-system writes are not implemented.

## Goal

Let Hearth send email, change a calendar, or write to another work system after the user confirms, and ensure the same operation is not executed twice. Prove it with a test account before touching a real account.

## How to do it

1. Generate a draft first; do not send directly. The draft can be edited, saved, or discarded.
2. The approval page must show the exact target, account, content, attachments, impact, and whether the action can be undone.
3. Approval is valid only for a short time and only once; nothing runs after the user declines.
4. Use a test account to complete the full flow: read, draft, approve, send or write, and receipt.
5. Keep a receipt after sending or writing; on failure, say whether the action may already have happened and how to check or recover.
6. Give the execution only the background information the task truly needs, and show which information was used.
7. Do not take over the user's main account without isolation.
