# 1.1 Define the first scenario and real tasks

## Current status

- **Done:** The 15 cross-industry tasks live in the standalone [hearth-eval](https://github.com/hearthproject/hearth-eval) repository's `eval/` directory and cover documents, the web, engineering, data/finance, customer support, HR, education, and marketing, with a fixed acceptance rule of at least 10/15 passes.
- **Actual result:** All 15 tasks ran and 13 passed. T07 and T08 remain open boundaries but do not block the hard threshold.

## Goal

Define who Hearth serves first and what problem it solves, then turn that work into 15 cross-industry real tasks that can be executed directly. At the end of this step, the team should be able to state the first user, the 15 tasks, what is explicitly out of scope, and what counts as done: at least 10 of the 15 tasks pass.

## How to do it

1. Find a first user who will provide real tasks and feedback, and record the repeated work they most want to hand over.
2. Use the 15 concrete tasks under [hearth-eval](https://github.com/hearthproject/hearth-eval)'s `eval/` directory as the baseline. Add candidates if real use reveals better work, but the MVP accepts these 15 tasks.
3. Do not require a single task family; the 15 tasks must cover document processing, web research, engineering collaboration, data/finance, customer support, recruiting, education, and marketing.
4. Write a one-sentence position and a not-now list so the first phase is not pulled in every direction.
5. Record how these tasks are done today, how long they take, and where mistakes happen.
6. Define MVP acceptance: at least 10 of the 15 real tasks pass, and failed tasks explain why and can be recovered.
